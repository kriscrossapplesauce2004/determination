01

Release.

SurfaceFlinger stops and its respawn paths are masked. The single-client composer HAL becomes available.

02

Acquire.

The guest compositor binds the display. EVIOCGRAB prevents Android and Linux from receiving the same input.

03

Return.

The guest releases cleanly. SurfaceFlinger restarts. Android gets its panel and input back.
